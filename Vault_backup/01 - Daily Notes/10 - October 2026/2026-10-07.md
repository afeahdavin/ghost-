## Leo Messi retirement note

As of 2026-10-07, Lionel Messi has retired from international football for Argentina. He announced it on 2026-08-31; his farewell match was 2026-10-06, a 3-0 friendly win over Benin at Estadio Monumental. In that match he played his record 208th senior cap, scored his record 126th international goal from a 71st-minute penalty, and assisted both other goals. He leaves Argentina with one World Cup title (2022) and two Copa América titles. He is still under contract with Inter Miami through the 2028 season.
